Vice Panels — an offline cover art maker for GTA VI launch week. Turn your own photos into a multi-panel Vice City poster: six poster layouts, six colour grades per panel, drag-to-crop each photo, layered caption lettering (Anton title, Pacifico script subtitle, launch badge), film grain, vignette and halftone — then export a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, 1920 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920. Photos never leave your machine, and the app makes no network request. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork, character or font: the lettering is Anton and Pacifico under the SIL Open Font License.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
